kirinuki — Privacy Policy

Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

kirinuki uses YouTube API Services.

Last updated: 2026-11-24

kirinuki is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users and has no public sign-up.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission through Google OAuth, kirinuki uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel;
  • read basic information about the owner's own channel and videos (titles, view counts, likes);
  • read analytics reports for the owner's own channel (views, watch time, average view percentage, subscribers gained).

No data from other users or other channels is accessed.

How data is stored and shared

OAuth tokens and analytics numbers are stored only on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared with, or sent to any third party, and are used only to upload videos and to understand which videos perform well.

Retention and deletion

Analytics numbers are kept for at most 200 snapshots and older ones are deleted automatically. All stored data, including OAuth tokens, can be deleted at any time by removing the local files. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

kirinuki uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

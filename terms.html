Last updated: 2026-11-24 · Privacy Policy

kirinuki uses YouTube API Services. By using kirinuki you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Who can use it

kirinuki is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • uploads videos that the owner made to the owner's own channel;
  • reads statistics and analytics of the owner's own channel.

Content rules

  • Every uploaded video adds the owner's own Japanese translation, subtitles and editing, and credits and links the original creator.
  • Any video is removed promptly if the original creator or rights holder asks.
  • Videos must follow the YouTube Community Guidelines.

Revoking access

Access to the YouTube account can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
